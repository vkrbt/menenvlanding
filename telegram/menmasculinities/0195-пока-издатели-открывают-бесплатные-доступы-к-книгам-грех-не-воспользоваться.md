Пока издатели открывают бесплатные доступы к книгам, грех не воспользоваться. 

Вот наткнулся на [Ecological Masculinities](https://tfstore.kortext.com/ecological-masculinities-384646?fbclid=IwAR06BagXbBg1fwq73j7HczrqW37-Lsnuslei21rinRNXqRSQLYzuRNHCxRY), и прикрепляю ее к посту. Полностью еще не прочитал, но то, что успел, довольно интересно.

"Around the globe, unfettered industrialisation has marched forth in unison with massive social inequities. Making matters worse, anthropogenic pressures on Earth’s living systems are causing alarming rates of thermal expansion, sea-level rise, biodiversity losses in terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems and a sixth mass extinction. As various disciplines have shown, rich white men in the Global North are the main (although not the only) perpetrators of this slow violence. This book demonstrates that industrial/breadwinner masculinities have come at terrible costs to the living planet and ecomodern masculinities have failed us as well, men included. This book is dedicated to a third and relationally focused pathway that the authors call ecological masculinities."
