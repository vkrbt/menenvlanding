In this 3-year project, a team of interdisciplinary researchers including psychological and sociological scientists from Poland (University of Gdańsk) and Norway (UiT The Arctic University of Norway, Tromso) will focus on the important, though understudied, topic of men’s participation in gender equality. We will analyse 1) how understanding gender roles and gender equality might help boys and men improve their well-being, and 2) which factors predict men’s participation in gender equality movements. This is a novel approach to the topic of masculinity and well-being, and we want to investigate it through different perspectives using suitable methods – focus group interviews, surveys, experimental studies and workshops conducted in schools and at universities (some of the studies will be conducted in Norway and Poland).We are looking for two PhD students/students: PhD student position 1: • MA in psychology & social, gender & cross-cultural psychology interests
 • Experience in participating in research projects (e.g. social psychology area or others)
