**Masculinity is in flux. **[**What does it mean to be a man in 2020**](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2020/oct/19/the-state-of-men-what-does-it-mean-to-be-a-man-in-2020-introducing-our-news-series-on-masculinity?CMP=Share_iOSApp_Other)**?Прошлые выпуски The Guardian ([сезон 1](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLa_1MA_DEorHnYu0HjO2pTwtulpJu2YAW); [сезон 2](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLa_1MA_DEorE9YESXIL1rN3jJ6qzaG40i)) на тему Modern Masculinity были довольно успешными, подняли в Британии (да и не только там) много вопросов о маскулинности и мужчинах. И вот, в 2020 уже Американский The Guardian не останавливаются, и запускают [новые серии о маскулинности](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/series/the-state-of-men), которая находится в движении:

“*As we started to conceive this series earlier this year, we brought together a diverse group of six male writers and social critics for a pre-Covid dinner conversation about what masculinity means to them. They discussed absent role models, abusive fathers and the heavy baggage of patriarchal beliefs. One participant observed, “As a black man in America, you’re constantly engaging in the tactics of releasing yourself of disadvantage and trying to then arm yourself with privilege. What I ended up realizing was that all that I was doing trying to be manly, was really just making me inaccessible to people around me.*”

И иллюстрации, которые используются в их материалах, лично мне доставляют огромное удовольствие.
