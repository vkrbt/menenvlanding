**15 PhD positions on biopsychosocial influences on gender during (diverse) hormonal transitionsThe Gender-InSIGHT Doctoral Network, a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Action (MSCA) of the European Union’s Horizon Europe research and innovation programme, is currently recruiting 15 Doctoral Candidates for an overall project **Investigating biopsychoSocial Influences on Gender during (diverse) Hormonal Transitions.The network brings together a range of disciplines including **psychology, history, ethics, endocrinology, neuroscience and public health.**Through co-creation and mixed methods research, individual projects will increase understanding of how biological, psychological, and social factors together shape experiences of hormonal transitions at different points in time and across different populations, including adolescence, menopause, in the general population and among people with variations of sex characteristics and trans and gender diverse individuals. 

15 Doctoral Candidates will be distributed across 3 lenses, each lens focusing attention on different aspects of hormonal transitions. A historical lens will document historical and ethical implications of the shifts in meaning given to biopsychosocial factors in the framing of hormones and gender over time. A clinical lens will provide clinical guidance informed by a biopsychosocial understanding of gender to assist patients and healthcare providers with decision-making regarding diverse hormonal transitions. A societal lens will uncover societal pressures and polarization regarding the biopsychosocial nature of gender and how these are communicated across cultures and through different channels.For more information** about individual projects, eligibility criteria, how to apply and application deadlines, consult the network website: [https://genderinsight.eu](https://genderinsight.eu/)
