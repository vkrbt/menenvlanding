A big day for Movember and hopefully for all those early career researchers in men's health out there looking for a unique funding opportunity to further their work and strengthen the field!

They are launching [Movember Community Research Program](https://movember.com/en/story/community-mens-health-research-program), an initiative **across the UK, Canada, Ireland and US (California) **where “we are seeking stellar researchers to partner with leading men's health community orgs on the ground doing game-changing work with men and boys to explore, evaluate and translate these programs into world-leading evidence.

This is the first of two multi-million dollar research fellowship initiatives we are releasing this year, our biggest investment yet into raising the profile of men's health research globally! We’re funding early-career researchers (≤5 years post-PhD) to be embedded within community organizations, focusing on one or more areas: 
 *👉*** How young men respond to pressure, adversity and what they turn to*👉*** Social connection*👉*** Early fatherhood. 

If you are a men's health researcher please register interest today, if you're a community org get in touch for support finding a researcher, if you're a leader in the field please share with your networks and help us find and fund the next generation of talent. 

Our aim is to find out what works and where and we want community organisations to have the best leg up, partnering with the best researchers we can find, to enhance and spread their work.”Key dates:April–May: Register your interest by [completing the form](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=Cz5MSVWONUK1LkwIXaQp9QgloIv7UUxNs_lEoPiGlVBUQ05KSFVNMTNHRUNNOE5JTU41MFJEQVIxTS4u). You will receive an email in May with full application details.
May 7, 2026: The Request for Applications (RFA) opens for early career researchers and remains open for six weeks. The deadline is 19 June 2026 at 5pm EDT / 10pm BST / 12pm PDT.
May 14, 2026: An information session will be held (and recorded) at 4pm BST for prospective applicants. Please register your interest [here](https://movember.zoom.us/meeting/register/Q1r7jK8KTBa8Mt8q7TH7jg).
